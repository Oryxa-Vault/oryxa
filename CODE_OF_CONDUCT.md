# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume the person you're replying to is smart and acting in
good faith, because they usually are.

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
v2.1 as its standard. What follows is what that means here in practice.

## What we want

- **Critique the work, not the person.** "This breaks replay because the log is
  the source of truth" is useful. "This is obviously wrong" is not.
- **Assume inexperience over bad intent.** Someone who doesn't know the
  conventions hasn't read them yet. Point at the file.
- **Let people be new.** A first PR that misses the mark is a normal first PR.
  Nobody arrives knowing how a project thinks.
- **Say when you don't know.** "I'm not sure this is right, but here's what I
  saw" is a genuinely useful contribution.

## What isn't welcome

Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning comments, publishing others' private
information, and sustained disruption. This applies in issues, pull requests,
commit messages, and anywhere else the project is discussed.

## Reporting

If something crosses a line, contact the maintainers through GitHub — open a
private security advisory on this repository, or reach out to
[@Shubham04-oss](https://github.com/Shubham04-oss) directly. Reports are handled
privately, and you will not be expected to argue your case in public.

Maintainers who don't follow this are held to it more strictly, not less.
